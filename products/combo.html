Why This 5-in-1 Combo Is Our Best Seller

Reetha & Shikakai: Gentle Cleansing

Reetha produces rich saponin lather while low-pH Shikakai gently conditions, detangles, and sweeps away impurities without stripping moisture.

Amla & Bhringraj: Follicle Vitality

Potent Vitamin C and carotene from Amla combined with the king of hair herbs Bhringraj deeply stimulate roots and curb premature greying.

Hibiscus: Lustre & Hydration

Rich in flavonoids and amino acids that seal cuticles, prevent split ends, and deliver long-lasting silken softness.

Application Ritual

  1. Master Herbal Hair Pack: Mix 1 tablespoon Reetha, 1 tablespoon Shikakai, 1/2 tablespoon Amla, 1/2 tablespoon Bhringraj, and 1/2 tablespoon Hibiscus with warm water or rose water into a smooth paste.
  2. Application: Part hair into sections and apply generously from scalp to ends.
  3. Rest & Rinse: Leave on for 20 to 30 minutes, then rinse thoroughly with plain water for healthy, vibrant hair.
